# References

Bibliography cited in the paper. Full texts are not redistributed here
(copyright belongs to the original publishers) — see licensing terms below
before downloading and keeping a personal copy.

1. Ahmad, T., Mourshed, M., & Rezgui, Y. (2017). Trees vs neurons: Comparison

   energy consumption. *Energy and Buildings*, 147, 77–89.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2017.04.038 (publisher, paywalled)

2. Amasyali, K., & El-Gohary, N. M. (2018). A review of data-driven building

   (publisher, paywalled)

3. Tsanas, A., & Xifara, A. (2012a). Energy Efficiency [Data set]. UCI
   Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C51307 (open,

4. Tsanas, A., & Xifara, A. (2012b). Accurate quantitative estimation of
   energy performance of residential buildings using statistical machine
   learning tools. *Energy and Buildings*, 49, 560–567.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2012.03.003 (publisher, paywalled)

5. Zhao, H. X., & Magoulès, F. (2012). A review on the prediction of building
   energy consumption. *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews*, 16(6),
   3586–3592. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2012.02.049 (publisher,
   paywalled)
